# 10 — Art: visitors and keepsakes

Status: waiting on the owner. Dependencies: none. Used by: 05, 06.

## What's needed

Three sheets in the storybook style, on the grey background the build tool expects:
- `assets/visitors-1.png`: birds (blue jay, northern cardinal, dark-eyed junco, American goldfinch, ruby-throated hummingbird)
- `assets/visitors-2.png`: animals and insects (cottontail rabbit, squirrel, chipmunk, green frog, red fox, bumblebee, luna moth)
- `assets/keepsakes.png`: Erwu's presents and the traces visitors leave

The visitors are small on screen (about a third to half of Erwu's height), so simple, clear shapes matter more than detail. Attach a photo of the real cottontail if you have one, as reference only (it is not stored in the repository).

NOT photographic, NOT 3D, NOT cartoonish.
>

> - Row 2: a northern cardinal (red male): perched; pecking at the ground; wings open, taking off.
> - Row 3: a dark-eyed junco (slate grey and white): standing; pecking; hopping.
> - Row 4: an American goldfinch (bright yellow and black): perched on a seedhead stem (draw the stem short); pecking; flying.
> - Row 5: a ruby-throated hummingbird: hovering with a blur of wings; hovering beak forward as if at a flower; perched on a tiny twig.
>
> Flat plain light grey background (#E6E6E6), with no ground, no shadows, no text, no grid lines, and wide empty space so nothing touches. Highest resolution.
