## Prompt

Attach: `.scratch/art-direction/target-mockup.webp` and `assets/garden-clumps.png`.

> A sheet of 16 separate flower clumps in EXACTLY the storybook illustration style of the attached clump sheet and mockup: simplified, gentle forms with soft warm-brown outlines, light airy watercolour and gouache washes, pastel palette (butter yellow, blush pink, lilac, cornflower blue, sage green, cream), warm afternoon light from the upper left, subtle paper grain. NOT photographic, NOT 3D.
>
> 4 rows of 4, one clump centred in each cell. All exactly the same scale as the clumps in the attached sheet, standing upright, seen from the same gentle three-quarter view from above. Each grows from a small tuft of grass at its base, with its roots at the bottom. The clumps are, in order:
> - Row 1 (flowers that grow from the buds in play): pink and cream tulips; a soft pink peony plant with two open peonies; red and orange field poppies; pale purple asters.
> - Row 2 (spring): purple and white crocuses; yellow daffodils; white snowdrops; a clump of forget-me-nots with a few tulip leaves.
> - Row 3 (autumn): pink sedum flower heads turning rust; tall grasses with fluffy seedheads; a small shrub with orange and red autumn leaves; brown seedheads of coneflowers.
> - Row 4 (winter): a hellebore with pale pink winter flowers; a sprig of holly with red berries; bare twigs with a little snow; a tuft of grass with frost and a little snow.
>
> Flat plain light grey background (#E6E6E6), with no ground, no shadows, no text, no grid lines, and wide empty space so nothing touches. Highest resolution.
