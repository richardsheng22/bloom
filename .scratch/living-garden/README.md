# A living garden

The garden grows over days and real seasons, has visitors while you're away, and grows in the style you play. The series was planned on 2026-09-29 and built through 2026-10-01. Its finished tickets (01–11), the measurements behind them and the review evidence are on the `archive/docs-2026-10-01` branch.

## What it is now

- **Days:** the garden grows a little every day on its own, and faster from play. Each day's first 25 turns count fully toward growth; later turns count less. (`garden-time.js`)
- **Seasons:** the real northern calendar sets the backdrop, the rose bed, which flowers bloom, and the light. Wild flowers turn autumnal from September and wintry in winter. When the season turns, half the annual wild flowers (daisies, cosmos, clover and the like) go to seed and drift off, leaving room to grow; the garden never drops below 40 plants. Filling the garden again in a season brings a note, fuller seasonal air with golden motes, and a few more butterflies.
- **Between days:** the wild flowers recede: 15% go to seed overnight, a fifth more for each further day away (up to five days), annuals first and most of all along the play ring, never below 40 plants. Their seed drifts off when you next open the garden. A day's play plants up to 25 new ones, so daily play keeps the garden full and a few days away leave visible room to grow. Beds and the rose bed never recede. (`garden-time.js`)
- **Visitors:** a North American cast (blue jay, cottontail, chipmunk, fox and others) comes by while you're away and leaves traces. Erwu leaves presents, and a blue jay's dropped feather can be picked up too; both go on her keepsake shelf. (`garden-visits.js`)
- **Your style:** each way of playing well sows its own flower ([ticket 13](13-a-garden-in-your-style.md)).
- **In a game:** four power-ups, trick shots off mushrooms, chains, stubborn buds and celebrations ([ticket 12](12-skill-shots-and-celebrations.md)).
- **Sound:** a synthesised soundscape of breeze gusts, fountain drips and crickets. (`garden-sound.js`)
